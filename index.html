Good questions

Frequently asked.

What’s not included in the price?

Domain, hosting and SSL (available as our optional yearly bundle, registered in your name), paid third-party tools or premium plugins, and stock photography or video. Copywriting and branding are available as add-ons.

How do payments work?

50% to start, 30% when the design is approved, and 20% before go-live. Prefer to pay once? Pay the full project fee upfront and take 5% off. Final files and access are handed over once the last payment is received.

What if I need to cancel or pause?

The 50% start payment covers discovery and design work and is non-refundable. Any milestone already delivered is billed. You keep everything delivered up to that point once it is paid for.

What if I want more changes than the revision rounds?

Each package includes fixed revision rounds (2, 3 or 4). Extra changes are billed hourly, and we quote the hours before starting so there are no surprises.

Who owns the website?

You do. You get the code, design files, content and all accounts. No lock-in.

How long will it take?

Roughly 10 working days for Basic, 4 weeks for Pro and 8+ weeks for Advanced. The biggest factor is how quickly content and feedback arrive — the quote builder shows an estimate for your exact selection.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. If you start on Basic or Pro and upgrade within 90 days, you pay only the price difference between the packages.

What happens after launch?

Every package includes free post-launch support (30, 60 or 90 days). After that you can add a monthly care plan covering updates, backups, security checks and small changes — Advanced includes the first 3 months free. SEO retainers are also available.

Can you work with my existing site or brand?

Absolutely. We can redesign, migrate content, or keep your existing branding. Tell us in the enquiry form and we’ll scope it.
