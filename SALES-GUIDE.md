# Guruva Infotech — Salesperson Guide (First Client Call)

**Goal of the first call:** understand the client's need, show the right package, and book a free 20-minute discovery call or get a proposal request. You are not closing a deal on call one.

---

## 1. Who we are (say this in 20 seconds)

> "Guruva Infotech builds fast, search-friendly websites for growing businesses. We have three clear packages with fixed prices, so you know the cost and timeline upfront. You own everything we build."

**What makes us different (pick one or two):**
- Fixed prices, no hidden costs
- Speed-first builds (Pro and Advanced target Google's "good" load time of 2.5s or less)
- Mobile-first and SEO-ready from day one
- Client owns the code, design and content, with no lock-in

## 3. How to run the call

1. **Open the pitch page** (`index.html`) on your screen. Press **Present** (or the **P** key) for slide mode. Arrow keys move between slides.
2. **Ask questions first** (section 4). Don't open with prices.
3. **Use the "Help me choose" quiz** with the client if they are unsure. It takes 30 seconds.
4. **Build the quote live** in the "Build your quote" slide: pick the package, add extras, show the total and delivery time.
5. **Close the call** with a next step: book the discovery call, or fill the "Your selection" form so the proposal is sent.

---

## 4. Questions to ask every client

1. What is the website for? (look credible / get enquiries / sell online)
2. Do you have a website now? What don't you like about it?
3. How many pages do you think you need?
4. Will you update content yourself?
5. Do you need online payments, logins or a custom app?
6. What is your deadline, and what is your rough budget?
7. Who else decides? (Get them on the next call.)

---

## 5. Pricing and payment rules

- **Payment split:** 50% to start, 30% when design is approved, 20% before go-live.
- **Pay in full upfront:** 5% off the project fee. This is the **only** discount you can offer.
- **Upgrade credit:** upgrade to a bigger package within 90 days and pay only the difference.
- **Not included in the price:** domain, hosting and SSL, paid plugins and tools, stock photos and video. We offer a yearly hosting bundle (Basic ₹5,999, Pro ₹7,999, Advanced ₹16,999 per year).
- **Optional monthly plans:**
  - Care plan: ₹1,500 (Basic), ₹3,500 (Pro), ₹8,000 (Advanced) per month
  - SEO Starter ₹10,000 per month, SEO Growth ₹20,000 per month
- **Revisions:** fixed rounds per package. Extra changes cost ₹1,000 per hour, quoted before we start.
- **Cancellation:** the 50% start payment is non-refundable. Delivered milestones are billed.
- **Ownership:** the client gets all code, designs, content and accounts once the final payment is received.

| "Will it rank on Google?" | "Every package is SEO-ready and Pro and Advanced add a full SEO setup. Nobody can promise rankings, but we build it right." |

---

## 7. Never do this

- Never promise Google rankings, traffic or sales numbers. The ROI slide is an illustration, not a guarantee.
- Never offer a discount other than the 5% upfront payment. Anything else needs the owner's approval.
- Never promise a delivery date before the discovery call. Timelines depend on how fast the client sends content and feedback.
- Never promise features outside the package. Add them as add-ons in the quote builder.
- Never share client details or a half-finished quote by chat. Send the proposal only after the form is filled in.

---

## 8. After the call (same day)

1. Send the client the pitch page link or file.
2. Record: name, business, package interest, budget, deadline, decision-makers.
3. Book the 20-minute discovery call if not already done.
4. Send the proposal request (use the "Send by email" or "WhatsApp" button on the form).
5. Follow up within **1 working day**.
